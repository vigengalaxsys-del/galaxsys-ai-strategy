01 · VISION

Why This Is Not Optional

The iGaming industry is moving faster than at any point in its history. Game cycles are compressing. Player expectations are rising. Competitors are already deploying AI across their pipelines. This document defines Galaxsys's response — not as an experiment, but as a company-wide operating standard.

"At Galaxsys, AI is not a feature we add. It is the way we work. Every team, every role, every deliverable will be produced with AI as a core instrument. There is no competitive future in which this is optional."
— GALAXSYS · SHARED COMMITMENT · ALL TEAMS

This is not about replacing people. It is about multiplying them. A designer using AI does the work of three. A developer using AI ships twice as fast with half the bugs. A product owner using AI sees patterns no human can see alone. We all benefit — and we all own this together.

02 · ROADMAP

The 5-Phase Transformation Roadmap

Each phase builds the conditions for the next — organizational, cultural, and technical. Transformation of this magnitude cannot be announced and wished into existence. It must be engineered.

1
Weeks 1–2
Owner: Leadership Team + Dept Heads
Leadership Alignment & Mandate Lock
Nothing succeeds without unified leadership. Every department head must publicly and privately align with the mandate before it reaches their teams. Silence from leadership is resistance by proxy.
  • 1
    Leadership team presents the AI mandate at an executive session with full business case — cost, speed, and competitive risk
  • 2
    Each department head signs an AI Transformation Commitment — formal internal agreement to champion, not merely permit, adoption
  • 3
    Assign an AI Champion per department: a senior individual contributor energized about AI who serves as internal advocate and troubleshooter
  • 4
    Establish an AI Steering Committee meeting weekly during the 90-day launch window — AI Champions, Dept Heads, and Project Manager team lead
  • 5
    Define the non-negotiable: any new project kickoff after Day 30 must include a declared AI toolset in its brief
2
Weeks 2–4
Owner: AI Champions + Team Leads
Discovery Sprint — Tools Audit Per Team
The company should not dictate every tool top-down. Empower each team to own part of the discovery. People adopt tools they helped choose — this creates investment and reduces resistance significantly.
  • 1
    Each team runs a 3-session AI Discovery Sprint: map workflows → identify AI leverage points → research and propose tools
  • 2
    Every team member submits their individual "AI Tool Proposal" — 3 tools for their specific role with a one-paragraph rationale each
  • 3
    AI Champions consolidate proposals into a Master Tools Matrix per department
  • 4
    Steering Committee approves the final Company AI Stack — Tier 1 (mandatory for all) and Tier 2 (role-specific)
  • 5
    Procurement initiates licensing for all approved tools within 5 business days of approval
3
Weeks 4–8
Owner: AI Champions + Optional Trainer
Structured Onboarding — Learning by Doing
Training that is theoretical fails. Every session at Galaxsys must be applied directly to real work. Teams learn AI by completing their actual tasks with it — no passive observation allowed.
  • 1
    Week 1: 2-hour hands-on workshop per department — every person completes one real task using their new AI tools
  • 2
    Assign "AI Pairing" — each team member paired with a peer for 2 weeks to share discoveries, prompts, and workflows daily
  • 3
    Create a dedicated Galaxsys AI Wins channel in Microsoft Teams where everyone posts daily examples of AI-completed tasks — celebrate output, not effort
  • 4
    Weekly 30-minute "AI Show & Tell" per department — one person demos how they used AI that week to normalize and accelerate peer learning
  • 5
    For struggling employees: 1:1 coaching session with AI Champion — supportive, never disciplinary
4
Weeks 7–11
Owner: Dept Heads + Project Managers
Integration into Process — AI Becomes Standard
Adoption is complete when not using AI becomes the exception that requires explanation. This phase formally embeds AI into every workflow gate — templates, retrospectives, OKRs.
  • 1
    Update all project templates, briefs, and delivery checklists to include a mandatory "AI Tools Used" field on every deliverable
  • 2
    Require AI tool usage discussion in sprint retrospectives and design critiques as a standing agenda item
  • 3
    Establish AI Usage Benchmarks per role — minimum expectations for how often AI is integrated in daily deliverables
  • 4
    Introduce AI productivity metrics into OKRs and performance reviews — as a growth indicator, not a punishment
  • 5
    PMs add an "AI Efficiency Review" to each project post-mortem: what was faster, what was learned, what changes next time
5
Week 11–12 + Ongoing
Owner: All Team Leads
Accountability, Iteration & Cultural Lock-In
Transformation stalls when attention moves on. This phase ensures AI usage becomes self-sustaining — embedded in how Galaxsys hires, evaluates, and promotes its people permanently.
  • 1
    90-day company-wide AI Audit: measure output velocity, cycle time, and AI integration depth per department — publish results internally
  • 2
    Update all job descriptions to include AI proficiency as a hard requirement — not a preference or nice-to-have
  • 3
    Add AI tool competency to the performance review framework for all roles across all seniority levels
  • 4
    Quarterly AI Stack Review — every 3 months, teams propose upgrades, deprecations, and new tool additions
  • 5
    Launch the Galaxsys AI Awards — quarterly recognition for the most impactful AI-driven innovation from any team or individual

04 · CHANGE MANAGEMENT

Handling Resistance — Reframes That Work

Resistance is predictable. It is not a sign of failure — it is a sign of transformation in progress. Below are the most common objections and the leadership responses that move people forward.

OBJECTION: "AI will replace my job."
The risk is not AI replacing you — it is a colleague using AI outperforming you. AI is a force multiplier for skilled people. Your expertise is what makes the output useful. Without your judgment, the tool produces noise.
OBJECTION: "The output quality isn't good enough."
AI output quality is directly proportional to the skill of the person using it. Poor prompts produce poor output. Your job is to become an expert at directing AI — a skill this company will invest in teaching you.
OBJECTION: "I don't have time to learn new tools."
You don't have time not to. Every week without these tools, a competitor closes the gap. Learning takes 2–3 weeks of real use. After that, you save more time than you spent learning. The math is simple.
OBJECTION: "My creative work can't be done by AI."
AI does not replace your creative vision. It removes the friction between your vision and execution. The best creatives in the world are already using AI to ideate faster, explore more, and produce more original work.
OBJECTION: "I'm worried about data security."
Valid — and taken seriously. The Steering Committee reviewed all approved tools for data handling. Any tool in our stack has cleared baseline security. Never input proprietary game IP into unapproved tools. Clear, enforced policy.
OBJECTION: "This feels forced."
It is. So was learning Figma instead of drawing on paper. So was switching to Jira. Every foundational tool shift feels forced at first — and then you can't imagine working without it. We are not apologizing for moving forward.
05 · GOVERNANCE

How We Measure This

What is not measured is not managed. The following framework ensures AI transformation is tracked and accountable — at company, department, and individual level.

🏢 COMPANY LEVEL QUARTERLY
  • Game production cycle time vs. pre-AI baseline
  • Number of game concepts explored per sprint
  • Bug rate: pre- vs. post-AI code review
  • Revenue per FTE vs. industry benchmark
  • AI tool adoption rate across all departments
🏬 DEPARTMENT LEVEL MONTHLY
  • % of deliverables with AI tools declared
  • AI tool utilization rate (active sessions)
  • Time saved per workflow type (estimated)
  • New AI use cases discovered per team
  • Training completion across department
👤 INDIVIDUAL LEVEL PERF. REVIEWS
  • AI tools actively used (breadth & depth)
  • Contributions to AI Wins Teams channel
  • Novel workflows shared with team
  • Output velocity trajectory over time
  • AI competency self-assessment, calibrated by manager
⚙️ STEERING COMMITTEE WEEKLY · 90 DAYS
  • Adoption blockers surfaced and resolved
  • Tool issues or licensing problems escalated
  • Resistance patterns identified and addressed
  • Quick wins documented and shared company-wide
  • Budget and procurement status review
